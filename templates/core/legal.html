Legal & privacy

Important product boundary

CertDesk organizes documents, records expiration dates, and sends reminders. It does not review insurance coverage, determine whether a certificate satisfies a contract, or provide legal advice. The workspace owner remains responsible for checking requirements with qualified professionals.

Privacy

Uploaded documents are available only to authenticated members of the workspace that owns the request. Public upload links are random, expire after seven days, and become unusable after a successful upload. Do not upload information you are not authorized to share.

If the operator enables AI-assisted metadata extraction, a workspace employee must explicitly request extraction after a file has been uploaded. The file is then sent to the operator-configured AI provider, and its suggested metadata is shown for review before being used. Vendor public uploads do not trigger AI extraction automatically. The operator is responsible for selecting a provider and documenting its processing and retention terms.

Data retention

Customers control their workspace data and may request deletion from support. Demo data is synthetic and can be removed at any time.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, use the support email configured by the product operator before launch.
